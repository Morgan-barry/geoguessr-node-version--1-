How to Play

Welcome to World Quiz!

  • Host: Create a room and share the 4-letter code.
  • Join: Enter a friend's code to enter their room.
  • Gameplay: You'll see a random street-view. Guess the location on the map.
  • Country Match: The fewer clues you use, the higher your score!
  • Precision Point: Guess the exact location for maximum points! The closer you are, the more points you earn.
